Heimdall is a 100% offline, privacy-first SMS firewall for Android. It silently intercepts spam before it reaches your system SMS database—no cloud servers, no accounts, and zero internet permission.

FEATURES

• Pre-Storage Spam Drop: Blocked messages never reach Android's system SMS database or wake your screen.
• 100% Offline: Zero internet permission requested. No data ever leaves your device.
• Anti-Evasion Defense: Automatically neutralizes hidden zero-width spaces spammers use to bypass filters.
• Custom Keyword & Regex Rules: Block any keyword, phrase, or regex pattern across any language.
• 1-Tap OTP Copy: Instant copy button on notifications with auto-dismiss.
• Stacked Sender Threads: Groups bursts from the same sender into compact monospace trees.
• Auto 30-Day Purge: Blocked spam is automatically cleaned up after 30 days.
• Smart Tags: Organizes clean messages into Bank, Card, Delivery, Travel, or OTP categories.

NOTE ON SENDING SMS

Heimdall is designed for reading and filtering incoming SMS, not composing new messages. To send SMS or RCS, switch back to Google Messages via the built-in shortcut in Settings.
